01 — Welcome

Day one.

Cloud Native Nordics

We are a volunteer community running cloud native events across the Nordics, and Cloud Native Denmark is our biggest one.

Three Danish cities, one organizing group. Everything is run by practitioners who do this work for a living.

From KCD to CND

We started as Kubernetes Community Days Denmark. As the programme grew past Kubernetes into platforms, reliability and the whole cloud native stack, the name grew with it.

All profits go to charity

840K

DKK donated to date

Why we do it this way

Nobody here takes a salary from this conference. Across the first three editions we have donated over 840,000 DKK, and 2026 is the year we pass one million.

  • 100% practitioner-led programme
  • Profit-for-charity governance
  • Community-driven sourcing
