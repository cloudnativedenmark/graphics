Dear speaker,

Thank you for speaking at Cloud Native Denmark 2026. The talks are what bring the community together, and we're glad yours is one of them.

This guide covers the practical bits, so you can focus on your talk. All times are Copenhagen time, CET (UTC+1). If anything is unclear, get in touch. We're happy to help.

## Speakers' dinner

> [!TIP] Wednesday 18 November, 18:00 CET
> **Kilden i Haven, Tivoli Gardens.** We'll be seated in the upper part of the restaurant.\
> A three-course dinner, drinks and Tivoli Gardens admission are included.

If you have signed up for the speakers' dinner, you should have received a ticket for it, sent around 14:40 CET on 12 November. The ticket is valid for the entire day (single entry).

If you'd like to be guided to the restaurant, some of the organisers will be at Tivoli's main entrance, and we enter at 17:40 CET.

## Your talk

There is a host for each track. They're your point of contact on the day, introduce you on stage and run the Q&A.

| Track | Thursday 19 Nov | Friday 20 Nov |
|---|---|---|
| Main track | Marcus Noble | Stefan Daugaard Poulsen |
| Breakout | Henrik Løvborg | Rasmus Selsmark |

> [!IMPORTANT] Before your talk
> Find your track host during the break before your talk, **at the latest 10 minutes before it starts**. If you can't find them, you can always go to the A/V table to get mic'ed up.

We're running a moderated Q&A after your talk: attendees send questions through Slido, and your host reads a selection to you. Let your host know if you don't have time for Q&A.

Your slot is on the schedule at [cloudnativedenmark.dk/schedule](https://cloudnativedenmark.dk/schedule).
