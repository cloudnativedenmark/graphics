## Audio and visual

### Talks and keynotes

- Bring your own computer and HDMI adapters for use during the talk.
- Format your slides in 16:9.
- Conference wifi is available. If you're planning a demo, we strongly recommend pre-recording it and having it ready.
- The room has a screen, a projector, and one microphone for each speaker or panelist.
- If you need any additional A/V, email [contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk](mailto:contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk) as soon as possible.

### Lightning talks

- The talk is 5 minutes, with slides in 16:9.
- Slides are presented from a shared computer and were due at [contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk](mailto:contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk) by 12 November. If you haven't sent yours, email them as soon as possible.
- There's no option to request additional A/V for lightning talks.

### Workshops

The workshop room has a projector, an audio system for the presenter, and seating for all 30 participants, with power for most.

### Before you start

- Check your computer and HDMI connection before the session: colours, contrast and format.
- Make sure your computer is on the wifi, or has another working internet connection, before you start.

## Your slides

- There are no requirements for the presentation template, but it must follow the [Code of Conduct](https://cloudnativedenmark.dk/code-of-conduct).
- Mind the resolution of logos. You'll find most of them on the CNCF landscape at [landscape.cncf.io](https://landscape.cncf.io/), or in the CNCF artwork at [github.com/cncf/artwork](https://github.com/cncf/artwork).
- Use the proper capitalisation of projects and companies, like "gRPC" and "containerd". The CNCF landscape is a good source here too, and the [CNCF style guide](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/style-guide.md) covers things like abbreviating Kubernetes.
- We'd like to make the slides available to the community and add them to the schedule, and we know some people like to look through them in advance. Please send us a copy of your slides as a PDF to [contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk](mailto:contact@cloudnativedenmark.dk) whenever you feel comfortable, company policy and time permitting.

## Dress code and Code of Conduct

There is no dress code for presentations, and we encourage you to be comfortable. That said, the [Code of Conduct](https://cloudnativedenmark.dk/code-of-conduct) applies on stage, both in how you appear and what you say. Please be tasteful and considerate in choosing your clothing, and keep in mind that we are a global community. Please don't wear shirts with global brand logos that aren't your own on stage. Solid colours (not white) work better than prints.

The Code of Conduct is strictly enforced. We ask speakers especially to review it, and to be inclusive in the words and images in their presentation.
